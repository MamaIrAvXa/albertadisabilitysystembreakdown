# OUTOFDATE — retire

**Archived 14 September 2026.** These are the previous versions of ADSB fillable forms that
have since been replaced. They are kept for the record, not for use. Nothing here should be
handed to a member, printed, or linked to.

The replacements live in `../september 2026 updates/`.

## Why each one was retired

**Three were retired for substance.** Their wording or their legal citations were wrong.

| Retired file | Replaced by | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `Fillable_Active_Application_Stop_Deduction_Letter.pdf` | v2.0, same name | Argued from "It is my understanding that the Government of Alberta has stated…" Replaced with four quoted passages from Alberta's own published material, each with its source printed beside it. |
| `Fillable_CDB_Underpayment_Repayment_Request.pdf` | v2.0, same name | Cited "section 8 of the Act" and "General Regulation section 10(1)". Both numbers are dead. Also carried two email addresses that no longer exist. |
| `Fillable_Underpayment_Notice_July2026.pdf` | patched, same name | Cited "Section 8 of the Act" twice. |

**The renumbering, for the record.** The underpayment power in the Act is now **section 6.2**
— it was section 8 and was renumbered by 2025 c20 s6; the Act runs 7 to 9 with no section 8.
The repayment duty is now **AR 96/2026 section 20(1)** — AR 91/2007 was repealed by section 22
of the new regulation. A worker who looks up the old numbers finds nothing.

**The dead inboxes.** `css.ascc@gov.ab.ca` and `css.iscc@gov.ab.ca` appear on contact pages in
some of these files. Neither is on alberta.ca. The Income Support Contact Centre is
`alss.iscc@gov.ab.ca`. The four zone inboxes are `northzoneaish@`, `aish.centralregion@`,
`calgaryaish@` and `southaish@`, all `@gov.ab.ca`.

**The other sixteen were retired for completeness, not error.** Their replacements are the same
form with plain-language guidance pages bound in front — what the form is for, what each section
is asking for, where it goes after it is signed, and what not to do. The form pages themselves
are unchanged and every fillable field is preserved.

## One thing this archive does not do

The copies still sitting in `pdfs/fillable/` are untouched, so every published link and QR code
still resolves. Retiring a version here does not take it off the website. Overwriting the file
at its published path is a separate, deliberate step.
