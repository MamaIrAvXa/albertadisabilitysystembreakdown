Heads up for anyone in the North Zone.

The AISH/ADAP email address has changed.

Old: northzoneaish@gov.ab.ca
New: ALSS.NorthZoneDIA@gov.ab.ca

The old one sends back an automatic reply telling you to use the new one. If you've emailed the old address lately, check your inbox (and your junk folder) for that reply, and send your documents again to the new address to be safe. Keep the auto-reply. It's your proof of the date you first sent it.

Worth knowing: alberta.ca still lists the old address on its own contact page, so don't be thrown if you see it there. Use the new one, and keep that auto-reply either way.

If you're in another zone and get a reply like this, send me a screenshot so I can post the new address for everyone.
