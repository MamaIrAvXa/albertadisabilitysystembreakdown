Illustrations — The Access Gap

Twelve illustrations. Yours to use.

These are the painted scenes from The Access Gap — Plain Language guide. Each one is built around a single message about how Alberta's disability systems work — or don't — for the people they were designed to serve.

Free to share. Free to print. Free to post.

Use them on protest signs. Drop them into social posts. Print them out. Hand them around. If an image speaks to your situation, take it. Nothing is gatekept here.
