The FCSSAA Resolution Package

Once a year, the Family and Community Support Services Association of Alberta votes on resolutions — formal asks that the whole association then carries to the Government of Alberta. We want one on disability income this year. This package is everything needed to make that happen in your town: the plain-language guide, the resolution itself, the full argument, and the people to send it to.

The deadline is real: noon, Saturday, September 26, 2026.
Resolutions go to director@fcssaa.org. The vote is at the FCSSAA Annual General Meeting on November 25, 2026. After September 26, we wait a full year.
The one rule that shapes everything. Only FCSS member programs and municipal or Métis Settlement councils can submit a resolution. Not individuals, not campaigns, not advocacy organizations however large. So the only way this reaches the floor is if a councillor or an FCSS board sponsors it — and the only way that happens is if they hear from us. One sponsor is all it takes.

  4. Who To Contact

    Where to send it

    Start with your own municipality — your own town's voice carries the most weight. Then work the warm list: councils that have already passed a pause motion or written to the Minister about the disability-income changes. They understand the issue already, which makes them the most likely yes.
