Ask your town to carry this — closes September 26

Once a year the FCSS Association of Alberta votes on resolutions it then carries to the government. We want disability income on that list. You and I cannot submit one — only a town council or an FCSS board can, so the whole job is asking them, and one councillor saying yes is all it takes. When income drops the need doesn't vanish, it lands on food banks, housing offices and the FCSS desk. That's their own logic; we're saying it back. About fifteen minutes, and after noon on Saturday September 26 we wait a full year.
